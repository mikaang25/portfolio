why choose us?

At Cuddle Fluff, we believe comfort should go wherever you go. That’s why we create adorable, high-quality bag-stuffy animals designed to brighten your day, spark joy, and add a touch of fluff to every adventure.

Vision

To create the best cuddly companion to Davaoeños.

Mission

Cuddle Fluff is a brand with a hint of gentleness that creates a companion in the form of a soft and cuddly teddy buddy that people can enjoy with ease and comfort. With resolute commitment to the psychological health and wellness of the people they serve. Cuddle Fluff aspires to become your source of comfort with a double purpose which is to be able to store small things that hold a sentimental value or just things you own in general. The product is locally produced for efficiency and easily available.
